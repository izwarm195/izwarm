**pivot** n./v. 枢轴；支点；转动、绕……旋转；（比喻）转向、调整方向

# Math Words

- **proportion** n. 比例 — *In a proportion, the cross products of the two ratios are equal, so 2/3 = 4/6 checks out because 2 × 6 = 3 × 4.*
- **variable** n. 变量 — *In the expression 3x + 2, the variable x may take different values, which is why the value of the whole expression changes accordingly.*
- **like terms** n. 同类项 — *To simplify 5x + 3y − 2x + y, we combine the like terms and get 3x + 4y.*
- **substitute** v. 代入 — *To evaluate 2x + 5 when x = 3, **substitute 3 for x** and compute 2(3) + 5 = 11.*
- **evaluate** v. 求值；求……的值 — *Following the order of operations, we evaluate 3 + 4 × 2 as 3 + 8, not as 7 × 2.*
- **inverse** n./adj. 逆；相反的 — *Addition and subtraction are inverse operations: to undo adding 7, we subtract 7 from both sides of the equation.*
- **reciprocal** n. 倒数 — *The reciprocal of 3/4 is 4/3, and multiplying any nonzero number by its reciprocal always **yields** 1.*
- ****yields**** v. 产生；得出（结果）（yield 的第三人称单数）— *Multiplying the two fractions yields 1.*
- **parentheses** n. 括号 — *In 2(3 + 4), the parentheses indicate that we should add first and then multiply by 2, **giving** 14.*
- **acute angle** n. 锐角 — *An acute angle measures less than 90°, so an angle of 45° is acute while 95° is not.*
- **right angle** n. 直角 — *A right angle measures exactly 90°, just like the corner of a square.*
- **obtuse angle** n. 钝角 — *An obtuse angle is greater than 90° but less than 180°, such as an angle of 120°.*
- **straight angle** n. 平角 — *A straight angle measures exactly 180°, so it looks like a perfectly straight line.*
- **translation** n. 平移 — *A translation slides every point of a figure the same distance in the same direction, without rotating or flipping it.*
- **dilation** n. 缩放；伸缩 — *A dilation changes the size of a figure by a scale factor, so a dilation of 2 doubles every side length while keeping the shape.*
- **permutation** n. 排列 — *The number of **permutations of three books** on a shelf is 3! = 6, because the order in which they are arranged matters.*
- **histogram** n. 直方图 — *A histogram groups continuous data into intervals, so we can see at a glance which score range occurs most often.*
