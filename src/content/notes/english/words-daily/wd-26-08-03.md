# Math Words
Stage 1 - Early Elementary
- **ordinal number** n. 序数（表示顺序、位置的数）— *"Third" is an ordinal number; it tells the position in a race.*
- **cardinal number** n. 基数（表示数量的数）— *"Three" is a cardinal number; it tells how many apples are in the basket.*
- **quotient** n. 商（除法得到的结果）— *In 20 ÷ 5 = 4, the quotient is 4.*
- **ascending** adj. 升序的（从小到大）— *Put 3, 7, 1 in ascending order: 1, 3, 7.*
- **descending** adj. 降序的（从大到小）— *The numbers 9, 5, 2 are listed in descending order.*
- **oval** n./adj. 椭圆形（的）— *An egg has an oval shape.*
- **curved** adj. 弯曲的；弧形的 — *A curved line bends smoothly instead of going straight.*
- **sphere** n. 球体 — *A basketball is a sphere.*
- **cone** n. 圆锥体 — *An ice-cream cone is shaped like a cone.*
- **cylinder** n. 圆柱体 — *A soup can is a cylinder.*
