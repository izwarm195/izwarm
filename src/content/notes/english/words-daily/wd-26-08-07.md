# Math Words

- **intercept** n. 截距 — *In the equation y = 2x + 3, the y-intercept is 3, so the line crosses the y-axis at the point (0, 3).*
- **domain** n. 定义域 — *The domain of f(x) = √(x − 2) is all real numbers x with x ≥ 2, because the radicand cannot be negative.*
- **dependent variable** n. 因变量 — *In the function d = 60t, the distance d is the dependent variable because its value depends on the time t.*
- **direct variation** n. 正比例关系 — *The cost C = 5n is a direct variation, since doubling the number of items n doubles the total cost.*
- **inconsistent** adj. 无解的（方程组） — *The system x + y = 3 and x + y = 5 is inconsistent, because no pair of values can satisfy both equations at once.*
- **power** n. 幂 — *In the expression 2⁵, the base is 2 and the power is 5, meaning 2 is multiplied by itself 5 times.*
- **radical** n. 根号；根式 — *The radical √9 simplifies to 3, whereas √(−4) is not a real number.*
- **polynomial** n. 多项式 — *A polynomial is a sum of terms such as 3x² + 2x − 1, and its degree is the highest exponent of the variable, which is 2 here.*
- **monomial** n. 单项式 — *A monomial such as −5x³ consists of exactly one term, so it contains no addition or subtraction signs.*
- **binomial** n. 二项式 — *A binomial such as x² − 9 has two terms, which is why the difference-of-squares formula applies to it directly.*
- **leading coefficient** n. 首项系数 — *In the polynomial 4x² − 3x + 7, the leading coefficient is 4, because it multiplies the term of the highest degree.*
- **like terms** n. 同类项 — *Only like terms can be combined: in 7a²b − 3a²b + ab, the first two terms merge into 4a²b while ab must stay separate.*
- **quadratic** adj. 二次的 — *A quadratic equation such as x² − 5x + 6 = 0 can have at most two real solutions, here x = 2 and x = 3.*
- **parabola** n. 抛物线 — *The graph of y = x² is a parabola that opens upward, and its vertex lies at the origin.*
- **completing the square** n. 配方法 — *By completing the square, x² + 6x + 5 = 0 becomes (x + 3)² = 4, which can then be solved by taking square roots.*
- **half-open interval** n. 半开区间 — *The inequality 0 ≤ x < 5 describes the half-open interval [0, 5), which includes 0 but not 5.*

  **centroid** n. 质心；形心（几何图形的中心，如三角形三条中线的交点）
  **spectrum centroid** n. 频谱质心（音频/信号处理中衡量频谱能量集中位置的频率值）
  **notion** n. 概念；观念；想法、看法
  **timbre** n. 音色（声音的质感特征，区别于音高、音量）
  **aperiodic** adj. 非周期的；无周期性的（不重复出现相同模式）
  **mesmerizing** adj. 迷人的；令人着迷的（美式拼写 mesmerizing，英式 mesmerising）
  **manifest** v. 显示；表明；显现 adj. 明显的 n.（船/飞机）舱单、货单
